# Trellis 2.0 Pricing And Validation

Status: working plan.

## Positioning

Trellis is CI for GTM motions. It helps GTM engineers define, validate, certify, and learn from GTM motions before bad inputs create bad data.

## First buyer

Start with AI-forward Series B/C companies that have at least one of:

- GTM engineer
- RevOps engineer
- technical growth operator
- AI SDR owner
- Clay-heavy or automation-heavy GTM workflows

Avoid teams that only want CRM cleanup or done-for-you outbound.

## Pilot offer

### Trellis Motion Infrastructure Pilot

Price: `$15k-$25k` for 30 days.

Scope:

- 1-3 GTM motions
- Motion Contract templates
- field registry setup
- Clay/source validation path
- one sequencer source
- one CRM/outcome source
- evidence pack after launch
- learning review

Success criteria:

- one motion is certified before launch
- Trellis IDs propagate into the execution system
- outcome mapping is defined
- evidence pack is useful to Head of Growth / RevOps / GTM Engineer
- customer asks to run the next motion through Trellis

## Recurring pricing hypothesis

- Team: `$999-$2,499/mo` for agencies/GTM studios
- Growth: `$3k-$5k/mo` for Series B teams
- Scale: `$7.5k-$15k/mo` for Series C/D teams with multiple motions and sources
- Enterprise: annual/custom for SSO, dedicated warehouse, advanced approval/audit, customer-owned warehouse

Implementation and connector work should be paid separately until repeated enough to productize.

## Validation bar before broad platform build

Do not build the full platform until Trellis proves:

```text
3 paid pilots
+ at least 5 certified motions
+ repeated request for preflight before launch
+ evidence packs used in a real GTM decision
+ at least one customer wants recurring motion governance
```
